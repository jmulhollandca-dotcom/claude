# SECTION 01 77 19 — STAGED HANDOFF AND PHASED OCCUPANCY

Project: [PROJECT NAME AND ID]. Rev 0, 2026-10-20. Bracketed values are filled per project.

## PART 1 — GENERAL

### 1.1 SUMMARY

A. This Section sets the procedure by which the Work is handed over in stages. Handover stages are defined by the occupancy areas and occupancy dates on the building permit. Within each occupancy area, a readiness ladder of stages H0 through H7 applies as set out in Part 3.

B. Related Sections: 01 31 00 Project Management and Coordination; 01 33 00 Submittal Procedures; 01 45 00 Quality Control; 01 77 00 Closeout Procedures; 01 78 23 Operation and Maintenance Data; 01 78 39 Project Record Documents; 01 91 13 General Commissioning Requirements; [Division 26 and 23 commissioning sections].

### 1.2 DEFINITIONS

A. Occupancy Package: one occupancy area as named on the building permit or a partial or phased occupancy permit, together with the rooms and systems assigned to it under 3.1.

B. Occupancy Date: the date for occupancy of an Occupancy Package as stated on the permit or on the AHJ-approved phasing schedule attached to the permit.

C. Handoff Stage: one of H0 through H7 in Part 3, at the grain stated for it.

D. Certificate: the Owner's standard form for the stage, listed in 1.5, submitted by the producer with the evidence the form lists.

E. Ack: the written confirmation by the named receiver that the stage has been received and is fit for its stated use. A stage is complete on Ack and not before.

F. Override: an Ack given with open items, recorded with the name of the person granting it, the reason, the date, and the open items by identifier.

### 1.3 TRIGGER

A. The Occupancy Date on the permit is the handoff date for its Occupancy Package. No other date substitutes for it.

B. The Contractor shall obtain the building permit's occupancy phasing, or apply for partial occupancy per area where the permit does not state phasing, within [30] days of award, and shall submit the permit and each occupancy or partial occupancy permit to the Owner's Representative the day received.

C. Where a permit is re-issued or amended, the Contractor shall submit the amended permit the day received. The earlier date remains on record; the amended date is a new entry.

D. The Contractor shall submit, within [14] days of award, a written notice if any Occupancy Date cannot be met, stating cause and recovery date. The same notice obligation applies at any later time, and the notice shall be given before the Occupancy Date passes.

### 1.4 HANDOFF RULES

A. Each handoff is a registered record with a producer, a receiver, the Certificate, the evidence, and the receiver's Ack. Verbal or email statements of completion do not constitute a handoff.

B. Each stage gates only the work that physically depends on it. No stage withholds work in another room, system, or Occupancy Package.

C. The Contractor shall submit each stage's Certificate the day the stage is finished. Grouping finished rooms or systems for a combined submission is not permitted. A finished stage not submitted will be recorded as held, with its age in days, and reported at each progress meeting.

D. The Owner's Representative will review each Certificate within [3] working days for stages H0 to H4 and [5] working days for stages H5 to H7, and will Ack or reject in writing with the failed criteria identified by line. A rejected Certificate is resubmitted complete.

E. The Contractor shall not consider silence as Ack.

F. A stage once Acked is not reopened. Defects discovered after Ack are handled under 01 78 36 Warranties and the Owner's issue register.

### 1.5 SUBMITTALS

A. Occupancy Package Map, within [21] days of award: each room and each commissionable system assigned to exactly one Occupancy Package, with the Occupancy Date from the permit. Rooms or systems not yet assignable listed as such with the document that will resolve them.

B. Staged Handoff Schedule, with the baseline schedule and each update: each H stage per room, system, and package, scheduled backward from the package Occupancy Date, with AHJ pre-occupancy inspections shown as predecessors of H6.

C. Certificates, one per stage per room, system, or package: Form H0 through Form H7 as issued by the Owner.

D. AHJ inspection certificates and occupancy permits, the day received.

E. Open items list with each Certificate, each item identified, dated, and owned.

### 1.6 DATE PROVENANCE

A. Every date the Contractor reports for a stage shall state who stated it, the document it comes from, and the date it was stated. Dates without a source will be recorded as hearsay and will not be used to measure the Contractor's performance in the Contractor's favour.

## PART 3 — EXECUTION

### 3.1 OCCUPANCY PACKAGE MAP

A. The Contractor shall assign every room and every commissionable system to one Occupancy Package. A system serving more than one package is assigned to the earliest package requiring it operational.

B. The Map is maintained current. Changes are submitted as a revision; the prior revision stays on record.

### 3.2 STAGE LADDER

| Stage | Grain | Name | Producer | Receiver | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H0 | Building | Enclosure | Contractor | Owner's Representative | Envelope closed, heat on, water test, field review, envelope punch list with dates |
| H1 | Room | Room release | Contractor | Owner's Representative, then following trades and OFCI | Room release checklist against room data sheet, lighting, power, fire protection or watch, access control, photos |
| H2 | Room | Equipment landed | Installer or OFCI logistics | Owner's Representative | All scheduled assets set, anchored, levelled; receiving reports; serials recorded; braces removed |

| H5 | System | Integrated systems test | Commissioning Authority | Owner | IST report at agreed load, failure scenarios passed, issues log closed or accepted with reason |
| H6 | Package | Occupancy and operational turnover | Contractor and Owner's Representative | Owner operations | Occupancy permit for the area, all AHJ inspections passed, all H1 to H5 inside the package Acked, punch list closed or accepted with dates, keys and credentials, O&M indexed, spares delivered |
| H7 | Building | Final turnover | Contractor and Owner's Representative | Owner | All packages at H6, final occupancy permit, closeout package complete against manifest per 01 77 00, Owner's written Ack |

### 3.3 PREDECESSORS

A. Within a room: H1 before H2 before H3 for systems in that room.

B. Feeding links: a distribution or head-end room's H4 precedes H6 of each package it serves.

C. Each AHJ pre-occupancy inspection for an area (fire alarm verification, sprinkler, life safety and exiting, electrical final, mechanical final, accessibility, and any the permit names) is a predecessor of that package's H6.

D. Priority, crew sequence, or schedule pressure never override a predecessor.

### 3.4 PARTIAL STAGES AND OVERRIDES

A. A partial release of a stage is permitted only as an Override, with the excluded scope identified by room, asset, or system identifier and a date to clear it. The exclusion is tracked as its own item and is a predecessor of whatever it blocks.

B. An AHJ inspection failure is reported the same day with the re-inspection date.

### 3.5 CLOSEOUT

A. H7 is complete on the Owner's written Ack of the closeout package per 01 77 00. Release of holdback follows H7 and the lien period, not the Contractor's submission.
